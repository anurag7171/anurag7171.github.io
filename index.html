About Me

A data and machine-learning practitioner based in Melbourne, focused on turning messy, real-world data into reliable insight and systems that actually ship.

My Journey

My path into data wasn't the obvious one. I studied Economics first and came to programming later, teaching myself the fundamentals from the ground up during my Master of Data Science at Monash. That gap turned out to be an advantage — it made me obsessive about genuinely understanding how things work, not just running the code.

Since then I've built end-to-end data and ML projects — from scraping and modelling data in SQL, through statistical analysis and forecasting, to interactive dashboards and deployed machine-learning models. I've also worked across fintech and financial analytics, which gave me a full view of how data flows from raw collection to real decisions.

Today I work as a Business Intelligence Analyst, and I'm focused on data and analytics roles here in Australia — building things that are accurate, reliable and genuinely useful.
